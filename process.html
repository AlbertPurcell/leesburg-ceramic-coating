How Ceramic Coating Works

Great ceramic coating is mostly prep. Here’s the process we follow so your finish bonds cleanly and lasts.

  1. 1

    Inspection

    We evaluate paint condition, existing swirls or oxidation, and how you use the vehicle. That drives package recommendation and correction level.

  2. 2

    Wash & Decontamination

    A thorough wash removes loose dirt. Chemical and mechanical decontamination clear bonded contaminants so the surface is ready for correction and coating.

  3. 3

    Paint Correction

    Light polish or multi-stage correction as needed to reduce swirls and refine clarity — matching Essential, Premium, or Ultimate goals.

  4. 4

    Ceramic Application

    Professional ceramic is applied in controlled sections for even coverage and strong bonding to prepared paint.

  5. 5

    Cure & Handoff

    Proper cure time, a final check, and a clear aftercare guide so you know how to wash and maintain hydrophobic performance.

Short FAQ

Quick answers before you book a quote.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Longevity depends on the system, prep quality, and how you maintain it. Our packages range from solid multi-year protection to high-end systems designed for longer service life — we’ll recommend based on your goals at quote time.

Is ceramic coating the same as wax?

No. Wax sits on the surface and wears off relatively quickly. Ceramic coatings form a harder, longer-lasting protective layer with stronger gloss and hydrophobic behavior when properly applied over corrected paint.

Do you work mobile or in a studio?

Service format (mobile vs. studio/controlled space) is confirmed at quote based on your vehicle, package, and schedule. Quality prep and cure conditions drive the recommendation.

Questions about the process?
