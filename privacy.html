Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Leesburg Ceramic Coating (“we,” “us”) collects through leesburgceramiccoating.com, our quote form, and our business phone number, and how we use it. We are a veteran-owned, mostly mobile ceramic coating business serving Leesburg and Loudoun County, VA.

Information we collect

Quote and contact form

When you submit the quote form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (optional), vehicle year, make, and model, ZIP code or neighborhood, and any message you write. We use this only to respond to your request, follow up about a quote, and schedule service.

Phone calls, call tracking, and call recording

We use CallRail, a third-party call tracking service, for the phone number shown on this website. The number you see may be one assigned to your visit so that we can tell which page or source led to your call. Calls to this number may be recorded and are tracked. CallRail may collect and share with us the caller’s phone number, the date, time, and length of the call, the call’s approximate location, the web page or source that led to the call, and a recording and transcript of the call. We use this information to answer questions, improve our service, and understand how people find us. If you do not want a call recorded, please tell us at the start of the call or contact us by the email address below instead.

Cookies and similar technologies

CallRail’s website script sets cookies or similar identifiers in your browser so it can show the right tracking number and connect a phone call to a website visit. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but the phone number you see may not be the tracked one. Some blog posts embed YouTube videos, and YouTube may set its own cookies when you play a video. We do not currently run advertising cookies or other analytics tools on this website. If we add them, we will update this policy.

Server logs

Our website host (Netlify) and form delivery provider (FormSubmit) may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested when you visit the site or send the form.

How we use information

  • To respond to quote requests and calls, and to schedule and perform work.
  • To follow up with you about your request.
  • To understand which pages and sources lead to calls and requests, and to keep the website working and secure.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (such as CallRail for call tracking and recording, Netlify for website hosting, and FormSubmit for delivering form submissions to our email), and where required by law. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

We keep contact details, call records, and recordings for as long as we reasonably need them to serve you, run our business, and meet legal requirements, and then delete or de-identify them. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
