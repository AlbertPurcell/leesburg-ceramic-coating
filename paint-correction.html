What Is Paint Correction?

Machine polishing that refines clearcoat — not a cover-up wax or spray detailer.

What it removes

Wash-induced swirls, holograms from poor polishing, light scratches, and oxidation haze that dull gloss under Virginia sun. Dark paint and black cars show these defects most clearly.

What it is not

It will not fix deep gouges into color coat or panel damage that needs bodywork. We set expectations during the paint check so you know what correction can — and cannot — achieve before ceramic.

Why Correction Before Ceramic

Ceramic coating is a protective layer. It preserves the surface you give it — good or bad.

Locks defects in

Coat swirls and they stay visible for years under a glossy hydrophobic shell. Correction first means you protect a clearer finish.

Better bond & gloss

Clean, refined paint helps ceramic wet-out and flash evenly — especially when cured in a controlled Loudoun/Ashburn bay instead of a dusty driveway.

Matches year-tier goals

Essential (~2–3 yr), Premium (~5–6 yr), and Ultimate (~8–10 yr) packages include the correction level your paint actually needs — not a fake one-size SKU.

Leesburg & Loudoun Wear

Local climate and roads create the defects we correct most often.

UV & summer heat

Strong VA sun accelerates clearcoat dulling and makes micro-marring more visible — especially on south-facing parking and Premium Outlets corridor lots.

Pollen & tree sap

Spring pollen and sap from Lansdowne, River Creek, and wooded Potomac Station streets etch if left on paint. Correction restores clarity; ceramic makes future cleanup easier.

Road grit & brine

Route 7, Dulles corridors, and winter brine introduce wash marks and bonded fallout. Decontamination plus correction resets the finish before multi-year protection.
