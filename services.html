Paint Correction Before Ceramic

Ceramic locks in what’s underneath. Swirls, haze, and oxidation should be corrected first — or you coat the defects in place.

When correction matters

Dark paint, lease returns with wash marks, and Loudoun cars that see Route 7 grit often need light polish or multi-stage correction before ceramic. We recommend level after inspecting your paint — never a one-size SKU.
